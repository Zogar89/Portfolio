Meli Core Dumper

From exporting data to producing intelligence inside the product.

Message me on LinkedIn View repository
Role
Product, architecture, backend and data
Constraint
External data and multi-tenant operation
Delivery
An operable decision platform

Analysis started where the product ended.

The original idea was to download the operation, export it to Excel and analyze it elsewhere. The data was available, but decisions still depended on manual work and scattered context.

The starting point

A full dump worked as a backup and raw material. It did not say what to look at first or which change deserved attention.

The product shift

If the system already had the data, it could analyze it where it was born. The dumper moved from extraction to applied intelligence.

The engineering was organized around three decisions.

  • Analyze inside the product

    Signals become the main output. Export stops being the end of the flow.

    01
  • Separate facts from signals

    First build a reliable operational base; then compute aggregates, patterns and anomalies.

    02
  • Make the operation visible

    Workers, checkpoints, snapshots and observability show what is fresh, what failed and what must continue.

    03

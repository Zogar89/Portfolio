Stock Central

A real friction in the 3D printing market, solved with an automated backend and a static runtime.

Message me on LinkedIn See the demo
Role
Product, ingestion, frontend and CI/CD
Constraint
Heterogeneous sources and minimal cost
Delivery
A 100% static community tool

Finding one color could mean asking supplier by supplier.

Filament mixes brands, materials, colors and pack sizes. With shortages and blocked imports, looking for stock became a repetitive task for buyers and makers.

The data existed

Some distributors published spreadsheets or pages with stock. The information was there, but fragmented and in different formats.

The product was missing

Bringing the sources together made search, comparison, order building and alerts about needed variants possible.

The architecture had to be cheap before it was sophisticated.

  • Move the backend into jobs

    The logic runs four times a day on GitHub Actions. There is no permanent server waiting for traffic.

    01
  • Separate connectors by source

    Sheets, supplier HTML and the official catalog are processed with their own rules and a shared output contract.

    02
  • Stop suspicious publishes

    Quality gates catch sudden changes, schema errors and abnormal drops before the public site is updated.

    03

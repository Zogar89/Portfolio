Review Monitor

Scattered reviews turned into a per-product tracking surface.

Message me on LinkedIn Back to projects
Role
Product, backend, sync and security
Constraint
No dedicated webhook in the API and shared entities
Delivery
Centralized review tracking

Mercado Libre had statistics, but not this view.

A seller could check metrics, but had no single place to read the reviews of their listings and follow how the perception of each product evolved.

The gap

Reviews carry qualitative signals that do not fit well on a screen of aggregate statistics.

The difficulty

The API requires polling and can share reviews across catalogs or product families. Centralizing without mixing products takes its own modeling.

The scope was narrowed to one precise question.

  • Track perception, not the whole operation

    The product focuses on what reviews say and how the perceived health of products changes.

    01
  • Sync at several cadences

    Daily polling, sync at login when data is stale and manual refresh make up for the missing webhook.

    02
  • Isolate accounts and entities

    Multi-account OAuth, renewable tokens and dedicated models avoid mixing users, listings and product families.

    03

A technical base to close the gap between reputation and product.
